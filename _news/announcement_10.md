New paper in *Ophthalmology*: [Large Language Model Authorship in Ophthalmic Publications](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ophtha.2026.05.037), finding that undisclosed LLM-generated text is increasingly present in the ophthalmic literature and arguing for clearer disclosure standards.
