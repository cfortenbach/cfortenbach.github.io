Presented at [ARVO 2026](https://www.arvo.org/) on the lab's work in photoswitch-mediated vision restoration: **Photochemical Restoration of Transient ON- and OFF- Retinal Ganglion Cell Responses**.

The talk covered our recent multi-electrode array recordings characterizing how photoswitch-treated retinas reconstruct transient ON and OFF response polarity at the level of individual ganglion cells — a key step toward understanding what kind of vision photoswitch therapy can produce.
