Christopher R. Fortenbach, MD, PhD is principal investigator of the Fortenbach Lab. He is a clinician-scientist and vitreoretinal surgeon at UW Medicine, with a research program centered on photopharmacologic vision restoration in retinal degeneration.

He completed his MD/PhD at the University of California, Davis, and then trained at the University of Iowa for residency and vitreoretinal surgery fellowship. He joined the UW faculty in the Fall of 2023.
