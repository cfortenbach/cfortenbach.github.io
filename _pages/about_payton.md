**Research Scientist**

Payton joined the lab from the UW Division of Rheumatology, where she spent nearly seven years as a research scientist and lab manager, leading subprojects on fibroblast and neutrophil biology. She brings hands-on expertise in animal handling, flow cytometry, qPCR, cell culture, tissue processing, and microscopy, and is now applying that toolkit to experiments on azobenzene photoswitches for vision restoration in outer retinal degeneration.
