**Medical Student Researcher**

Hayden is a medical student at the University of Washington School of Medicine completing an Independent Investigative Inquiry (Triple I) research project in the lab. Triple I is the UW School of Medicine scholarly project requirement, in which students carry out a mentored research project over the summer between their first and second years and present their findings the following fall. Hayden is studying the mechanisms of novel photoswitches for vision restoration in outer retinal degeneration.
